# Prompt: Draft LinkedIn Messages

Goal: write connect notes and first messages that feel like a real person reaching out — not a recruiter template, not a pitch.

Read `config/writing_samples.md` before drafting. Match the writing style.

---

## Tone rules

Same as email, see `draft_email.md`. Additionally:
- No dashes of any kind as separators (`-`, `—`). Use a comma or a period instead.
- LinkedIn connect notes are max 300 characters. Count them.
- First message (after connect) should be under 80 words.
- Sound curious, not desperate. You're exploring, not begging.
- Mention something specific about them or the company — not generic.
- No "I came across your profile and was impressed by..." — everyone uses that.

---

## Connect note (HR / recruiter) — ≤300 chars

Template direction:
- State who you are in one phrase (e.g., "software engineer", "backend engineer with X years")
- Say why you're reaching out to them specifically (interest in the company, not "looking for any job")
- Ask nothing. The note isn't a pitch — it's just an intro.

Example style (do NOT copy verbatim, write fresh each time):
```
hey! im an [Softwere engineer / Android engineer / Mobile engineer] and i really like what you're doing with [product/what they build]. Would love to connect and hear if there's anything relevant on your end.
```

---

## Connect note (team lead / engineering manager) — ≤300 chars

- Lead with something technical or product-related, not "I want a job".
- Show you know something about their work or the company's stack/product.

Example style:
```
hey! i work on [relevant tech/domain] and have been interested in [company]'s approach to [something specific]. 
wanted to connect and chat if you're open to it.
```

---

## First message (after connect accepted)

Keep it short — 2-3 sentences max.

For HR:
```
thanks for connecting! i'm actively looking for my next role and [company] is definitely on my shortlist. 
i sent a note to your careers email as well — happy to jump on a quick call if that's easier.
```

For team lead:
```
thanks for connecting! i've been curious about [something specific they work on or that the company is known for]. 
i'm currently exploring new roles — would love to grab 20 mins to hear about the team if you're open to it.
```

---

## What NOT to write

- "I came across your profile"
- "I am a highly motivated..."
- "I would be a great fit"
- "I'd love the opportunity"
- "Please let me know if..."
- Anything with a bullet list
- Anything over 300 chars for connect note (hard limit)
