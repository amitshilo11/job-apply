# Prompt: Draft Email

Goal: write a cold outreach email that sounds like a real person wrote it — not an AI, not a recruiter, not a cover letter.

Read `config/writing_samples.md` before drafting. Match the rhythm, vocabulary, and length of those samples.

---

## Tone rules (non-negotiable)

- Short. Under 150 words for the body. If you can say it in 80, do that.
- Lowercase "i" is fine occasionally (not every time, just naturally).
- Contractions always: "I'm", "it's", "don't", "I've" — never "I am", "it is".
- No dashes of any kind as separators (`-`, `—`). Use a comma or start a new sentence instead. No semicolons. No bullet lists in the email.
- One or two natural imperfections: a missing comma, a word repeated lightly, "tbh", "honestly", one sentence that's slightly informal. Don't overdo it, one or two max.
- No AI tells. Never use:
  - "I'm excited to..."
  - "I'd love the opportunity to..."
  - "I am writing to express my interest..."
  - "I am a passionate..."
  - "Please find attached..."
  - "I look forward to hearing from you"
  - "Best regards" (use just the name, or "thanks," or "cheers,")

---

## Structure: role-specific email

```
Subject: <role title> / <candidate first name>

Hey [name / team],

saw the [role title] opening and wanted to reach out. [1 sentence: why this company specifically — something real, not generic "I admire your mission".]

[1-2 sentences: most relevant thing about you for this role. Be specific, not vague.]

CV attached. happy to chat if it's a fit.

[first name]
```

---

## Structure: general intro email (no open role)

```
Subject: software engineer / <candidate first name>

Hey [name / team],

not sure if you're hiring right now but wanted to reach out. [1 sentence about why this company specifically.]

[1-2 sentences: what you do / what you've built, most relevant to what they do.]

cv's attached either way. [optional: "would be great to stay in touch"]

[first name]
```

---

## Follow-up email (circling back after ~5 days of silence)

```
Subject: Re: <original subject>

hey, just circling back on this — no worries if the timing's off, just wanted to make sure it didn't get buried.

[first name]
```

Keep it under 3 sentences. Never beg or over-explain.

---

## Final check before saving

- Read it out loud (mentally). Does it sound like a human?
- Count sentences in each paragraph — no paragraph over 2 sentences.
- Remove any word that sounds like it came from a template.
